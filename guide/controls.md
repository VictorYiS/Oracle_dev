# Controls

**English** · [中文](controls.zh-CN.md)

You are the Interpreter. You sit at a window on the street and decide what people ask for; you can also leave the window and go and see things for yourself. Time runs only in the daytime.

## The day

- **Dawn** and **morning**: nothing moves. Press **Enter** (or the button in the top bar) to go on.
- **Daytime**: the clock runs. **Space** pauses and resumes, **1 / 2 / 3** set the speed (×1, ×2, ×4), **T** lets an hour pass.
- **Dusk**: work ordered during the day is carried out; whoever is still waiting at the window goes home. Then night, then the next dawn.

## Walking

- **A / D** or **← / →**: walk along the layer you are on (the street, the riverbank, the far bank, a room).
- **Click the ground** to walk there; click something on your layer to walk to it and use it.
- **↑ / W**: take a nearby passage inward, including steps, bridges, alleys and doors. **↓ / S** takes a passage outward. The hint names the available passage and its key.
- **E**: use the nearby window or notice.
- On a phone: hold **◀ ▶** to walk, tap **↑ ↓** for passages, **E** to use an object.

Walking takes game time, and so does every passage: the steps down to the river, the footbridge, the alley, a door.

## Looking is not going

The street is a paper theatre: cards standing at different depths. Distant buildings grow as you approach their layer; rooms use a closer view that follows you.

- Stand beside a bookcase in the registry or 17B. **Wheel up** or **F** inspects it. **You stay where you are.**
- **Wheel down** or **Esc** returns to the character. **↑ / ↓** also leaves inspection before you can travel.
- On a phone: **⊕** inspects, **⊖** returns. The buttons are available only when you can use them.
- Ordinary street scrolling does not move the camera. Zoom is available only at designated objects.

The top-left corner always says where *you* are and where the camera is *looking*. Clicking something on a far layer does nothing: to get there, walk, and take the passages.

## Seen, remembered, and drawn by the Oracle

- A building you have never seen is drawn by the Oracle, from the institutions' records: clean, pale and glowing. When the records change, its drawing follows.
- Stand near a building and you see it as it is, in ink.
- Walk away and it stays as you last saw it, a memory that slowly fades. The town goes on changing behind your back; your memory does not. Come back, and if anything changed, it is redrawn and the event feed tells you what is different.

## The window

Press **E** at the window. Visitors queue there in the daytime.

- **Papers**: the visitor's application and an extract from each institution concerned. Every paper says where it came from and when. Open one to read it close up.
- **Procedure**: the rules for this kind of case.
- **The Oracle suggests**: a one-line summary and an action. **Adopt** does what it suggests; or stamp a decision yourself.
- Decisions that need work (a connection, a repair) are carried out at dusk.

## Pending checks

When you adopt a suggestion, what the Oracle took for granted goes on your tray (**P**, or the tab at the top right), with how to check it: go and look, or read the original papers at the window (which takes a while). Checking settles it as right or wrong. Nothing is held against you either way.

## Settings

**Settings** in the top bar has **Reduce motion**: the camera cuts instead of moving, and nothing flickers or bobs. **?** lists every key.
